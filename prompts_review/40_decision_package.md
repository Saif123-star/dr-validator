# Verified Findings Delivery

Present the function key and review baseline, evidence availability and
limitations, coverage by track, function map, and every verified or
reconciled finding. For every finding, include its stable ID, severity,
confidence, impact, evidence and locators, recommendation, verification
verdict, and reconciliation status.

Generate the local review report immediately after verification and any
required reconciliation. Do not pause for acceptance or require a human
disposition before producing the report. DR edits and external writes are
never performed.
