# Track C — DR Internal Consistency and Readiness

Check for contradictions between sections and between controls/events,
screen-mode mismatches, inconsistent validation and success/error behavior,
on-load/search differences, naming drift, ambiguity, non-testable statements,
missing trigger/result/error behavior, unclear defaults, unresolved
placeholders, and cross-section duplication.

Compare the DR page and its available workbook sheets where they describe the
same behavior. Trace each applicable rule through its trigger, preconditions,
state transitions, permissions, defaults, and success/error outcomes. Note
which sections and sheets you checked and which could not be assessed. Leave
the systematic field-length, component, and endpoint inventories to technical
mapping; do not skip a contradictory business rule just because it has a
technical consequence.

State the two or more conflicting locators in the evidence. A requirement
document may describe a deliberate open question; distinguish that from an
accidental contradiction.
