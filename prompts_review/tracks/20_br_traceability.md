# Track B — BR Traceability and Business Completeness

Compare each actionable DR rule against the supplied BR. Identify missing BR
requirements, unsupported DR requirements, unresolved BR questions, and
coverage gaps for pre/post conditions, roles, modes, validations/messages,
business statuses, menu paths, and screen flow.

Produce a requirement traceability matrix with requirement ID or locator, BR
evidence, DR evidence, and result (`covered`, `missing`, `unsupported`,
`unclear`, or `not_applicable`). Cite exact source quotes in findings. Do not
invent a requirement to fill a gap.
